## 1. Overview and Registration Pathway Recommendation

### 1.1 Bangladesh NGO Registration Frameworks

Bangladesh offers several legal frameworks under which a civil society or non-profit organization can be formally constituted. The three most relevant for Blu Foundation are:

| Framework | Governing Law | Suitable For |
|---|---|---|
| **Society** | Societies Registration Act 1860 | Membership-based non-profits; simplest route for domestic operations |
| **NGOAB-registered Organization** | Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act 2016 | Any organization receiving or intending to receive foreign funds |
| **Company Limited by Guarantee** | Companies Act 1994 | Larger organizations requiring corporate governance structure |

### 1.2 Recommended Pathway for Blu Foundation

Given that Blu Foundation is an education-focused NGO at the formation stage, the recommended standard pathway is a **two-step process**:

**Step 1 — Society Registration**
Register as a Society under the **Societies Registration Act 1860** with the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC). This establishes the legal entity.

**Step 2 — NGOAB Registration** *(conditional — see flag below)*
Register with the **NGO Affairs Bureau (NGOAB)** under the **Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act 2016**. This step is required only if Blu Foundation intends to receive any foreign donations, grants, or funding from international sources.

**Domestic-only funding:** If Blu Foundation will operate exclusively on domestic Bangladeshi funding (local donations, government grants, Bangladeshi corporate CSR), Society registration alone under the Societies Registration Act 1860 is sufficient for initial operations.

**Alternative pathway:** Registration as a company limited by guarantee under the **Companies Act 1994** provides a more structured corporate governance framework and may be preferable if Blu Foundation anticipates significant scale, formal board accountability requirements, or engagement with institutional funders who prefer a company structure. This route is more complex and costly at the outset but provides structural advantages at scale. [VERIFY SUITABILITY WITH LEGAL COUNSEL]

### 1.3 Founder Input Required

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Will Blu Foundation seek foreign donations, grants, or funding from international organizations (e.g., UN agencies, foreign government aid programs, international foundations, overseas diaspora donations)? This is the single most important determinant of whether NGOAB registration is required.

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Is the founding principal a Bangladeshi citizen? Foreign nationals founding or directing an NGO in Bangladesh are subject to additional approval requirements from the Ministry of Home Affairs and NGOAB. This materially affects the registration timeline and process.

## 2. Pre-Registration Checklist

Complete all items in this checklist before beginning any formal filing. Incomplete preparation is the most common cause of delays.

### 2.1 Organizational Identity

- [ ] **Choose the organization name** — Confirm "Blu Foundation" or a full Bangladeshi legal name variant is available and not already registered with RJSC. The name must be unique.
  - Action: Search RJSC records to confirm name availability [VERIFY search method with RJSC directly or via legal counsel]
  - The official name on registration documents must be used consistently across all subsequent filings
- [ ] **Finalize the Mission Statement** — A concise written statement of what Blu Foundation does and why (one to three sentences). This will appear in the Memorandum of Association.
- [ ] **Finalize the Vision Statement** — A forward-looking statement of the long-term change Blu Foundation seeks to achieve.
- [ ] **Confirm the stated objects of the society** — These are the specific purposes for which the organization is formed, as listed in the Memorandum of Association. For Blu Foundation: manners and etiquette education for primary school children in Bangladesh. The objects clause must be drafted carefully — it determines what the organization is legally permitted to do.

### 2.2 Founding Members

- [ ] **Identify a minimum of 7 founding members** — Society registration under the Societies Registration Act 1860 requires a minimum of 7 founding members (natural persons).
  - All 7 must be willing to sign the founding documents
  - All must be adults with valid national identification
- [ ] **Collect from each founding member:**
  - [ ] Full legal name (as on National Identity Document)
  - [ ] Current residential address
  - [ ] Occupation
  - [ ] National Identity Document (NID) number and photocopy
  - [ ] Recent passport-size photographs (confirm number required [VERIFY WITH RJSC])
  - [ ] Signature (wet ink will be required on filed documents)

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Provide the names and NID details of all founding board members. Minimum 7 individuals required. This information is needed to draft the founding member list and Memorandum of Association.

### 2.3 Registered Office

- [ ] **Identify and secure a physical registered office address in Bangladesh** — A real, physical address is required. A P.O. box is not sufficient.
- [ ] **Prepare proof of address** — This may be a rental agreement, utility bill, or property ownership document in the organization's name or an authorized name. [VERIFY acceptable proof formats with RJSC and NGOAB]

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Provide the registered office address. This address will appear on the registration certificate and all official correspondence.

### 2.4 Governing Documents

- [ ] **Draft the Memorandum of Association** — covering: name of the society, registered office location, and objects of the society
- [ ] **Draft the Rules and Regulations (Constitution)** — covering at minimum:
  - Membership criteria and classes
  - Governing body (executive committee) structure, composition, and term limits
  - Election procedures for office bearers
  - Meeting frequency requirements (general body and executive committee)
  - Quorum requirements
  - Financial management procedures
  - Audit requirements
  - Amendment procedures
  - Dissolution provisions
- [ ] **Legal review of governing documents** — Have a qualified lawyer review and finalize both documents before signing or filing

### 2.5 Professional Support (Pre-Registration)

- [ ] Identify and engage a lawyer with NGO/society registration experience in Bangladesh
- [ ] Identify and engage a chartered accountant for compliance planning

## 3. Part A: Society Registration (Societies Registration Act 1860)

**Authority:** Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC)
**Legal basis:** Societies Registration Act 1860

### 3.1 Step-by-Step Registration Process

1. **Prepare the Memorandum of Association**
   - Include: full name of the society, address of the registered office, and a clear statement of the objects (purposes) of the society
   - Have all founding members review and approve the draft
   - Prepare 3 printed copies (confirm current copy requirement with RJSC)

2. **Prepare the Rules and Regulations**
   - The governing document of the society (see Section 2.4 for required content)
   - Prepare 3 printed copies (confirm current copy requirement with RJSC)

3. **Prepare the founding member list**
   - Typed list containing: full name, residential address, occupation, and signature of each founding member
   - Minimum 7 founding members required
   - All members must personally sign the list (wet ink signatures required)

4. **Prepare all supporting documents** (see document list in Section 3.2 below)

5. **Complete the application form**
   - Obtain the current application form from RJSC directly or through the RJSC online portal
   - [VERIFY WITH RJSC whether an online filing option is available or if in-person submission is required]

6. **Submit the application to RJSC**
   - Submit at the RJSC office or via the applicable online portal
   - Retain proof of submission (acknowledgement receipt)

7. **Pay the registration fee**
   - [VERIFY CURRENT FEE WITH RJSC] — fees are set by government schedule and subject to change
   - Retain the official payment receipt

8. **Respond to any RJSC queries**
   - RJSC may request additional information, corrections, or clarifications
   - Respond promptly to avoid delays in processing

9. **Receive the Society Registration Certificate**
   - This is the primary legal document establishing Blu Foundation as a registered entity
   - Store the original securely; obtain certified copies

10. **Obtain certified copies of the registration certificate**
    - Multiple certified copies are needed for subsequent steps (bank account, TIN, NGOAB)
    - [VERIFY WITH RJSC the process and cost for obtaining certified copies]

### 3.2 Documents Required for Society Registration

- [ ] Completed application form (form prescribed by RJSC)
- [ ] Memorandum of Association (3 copies, signed by all founding members)
- [ ] Rules and Regulations / Constitution (3 copies, signed by all founding members)
- [ ] Founding member list (with names, addresses, occupations, and wet ink signatures)
- [ ] NID photocopies of all founding members (attested/notarized as required)
- [ ] Passport-size photographs of all founding members
- [ ] Proof of registered office address (rental agreement, utility bill, or ownership document)
- [ ] Payment receipt for registration fee

> **[VERIFY WITH RJSC]** Confirm the current and complete document list, number of copies required, attestation/notarization requirements, and whether any documents require affidavit or commissioner of oaths certification before submission.

### 3.3 Timeline

Estimated processing time: **approximately 4–8 weeks** from submission of a complete application.

> **[VERIFY CURRENT TIMELINE WITH RJSC]** Processing times vary based on RJSC workload, completeness of submission, and any queries raised. Budget additional time for responding to queries or correcting deficiencies.

### 3.4 Part A Completion Checklist

- [ ] Memorandum of Association drafted, reviewed by lawyer, and signed by all founding members

## 4. Part B: NGOAB Registration (for Organizations Seeking Foreign Donations)

**Authority:** NGO Affairs Bureau (NGOAB), Prime Minister's Office
**Legal basis:** Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act 2016

> **This section applies only if Blu Foundation intends to receive foreign donations or funding from foreign sources.** If Blu Foundation will operate on domestic funding only, proceed directly to Section 5 (Bank Account Requirements).

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Confirm whether NGOAB registration is required before beginning the steps in this section.

### 4.1 Step-by-Step NGOAB Registration Process

1. **Obtain Society Registration Certificate** (prerequisite — must complete Part A first)
   - NGOAB registration cannot proceed without a valid Society registration certificate (or equivalent legal entity registration)

2. **Open an organizational bank account** (prerequisite — see Section 5)
   - A bank account statement will be required for the NGOAB application
   - Complete Section 5 before proceeding to Step 4 of this section

3. **Obtain Tax Identification Number (TIN)** (prerequisite — see Section 6)
   - TIN certificate is required for NGOAB application
   - Complete Section 6 before proceeding to Step 4 of this section

4. **Register on the NGOAB online portal**
   - Access the NGOAB online application system
   - [VERIFY CURRENT PORTAL URL WITH NGOAB DIRECTLY — do not rely on third-party sources for this link]
   - Create an organizational account on the portal

5. **Prepare the project proposal**
   - NGOAB registration requires a project proposal describing:
     - Planned activities (manners and etiquette education programs for primary school children)
     - Target beneficiaries and geographic areas
     - Implementation methodology
     - Monitoring and evaluation approach
     - Proposed budget (typically a 3-year projection)
     - Funding sources (domestic and foreign)
   - The proposal must be realistic, detailed, and consistent with the stated objects in the Memorandum of Association

6. **Prepare a 3-year organizational budget**
   - Itemized income and expenditure projections
   - Must account for both program and administrative costs
   - Recommended: prepared or reviewed by a chartered accountant

7. **Compile all required documents** (see Section 4.2 below)

8. **Submit the application via the NGOAB portal**
   - Upload all required documents in the formats specified by the portal
   - Retain the application submission reference number

9. **Government inspection and verification**
   - NGOAB will conduct a physical inspection visit to the registered office address
   - Ensure the registered office is operational and accessible at the time of inspection
   - Key office bearers should be available to meet the inspection team

10. **Ministry of Social Welfare review and approval**
    - For education-focused NGOs, the primary line ministry is typically the Ministry of Social Welfare or Ministry of Primary and Mass Education
    - [VERIFY WITH NGOAB which ministry provides the relevant ministerial clearance for education NGOs at the time of application]
    - The ministerial approval is a required step before NGOAB issues the registration certificate

11. **Receive NGOAB Registration Certificate**
    - This certificate must be maintained and renewed as required
    - [VERIFY WITH NGOAB the current renewal period and process]

Obtain the official checklist from NGOAB before preparing the application.

### 4.3 Critical Post-Registration Compliance Requirements (NGOAB)

Once NGOAB registration is obtained, the following obligations apply. **Non-compliance can result in suspension or cancellation of registration:**

- [ ] **Separate bank account for foreign funds** — Foreign donations must be received into and disbursed from a designated bank account. Foreign funds cannot be commingled with domestic funds.
- [ ] **Project-level approval before receiving foreign funds** — Each project funded by foreign donations requires a separate NGOAB approval before funds can be received. Registration alone does not authorize receipt of any specific foreign grant.
- [ ] **Annual report submission to NGOAB** — Submit an annual activity report and financial report to NGOAB by the required deadline each year [VERIFY CURRENT DEADLINE]
- [ ] **Project completion reports** — Submit project-specific completion reports to NGOAB upon conclusion of each approved project
- [ ] **Audit requirement** — Annual accounts must be audited by a qualified chartered accountant; audit report submitted to NGOAB as required
- [ ] **Retention of records** — Maintain financial records and project documents for the period specified by NGOAB regulations [VERIFY CURRENT RETENTION PERIOD]
