## 1. Website Objectives

The Blu Foundation website is not a brochure. It is a working tool — the primary point of contact between the organisation and every audience it must win over: the school administrator weighing a partnership, the parent curious about the programme, the donor deciding where to direct funds, and the journalist looking for a credible source. Every page, paragraph, and call to action must serve one of the three objectives below.

### 1.1 Primary Objective — Establish Credibility

Blu Foundation operates in a space — values education and social-emotional learning for primary school children — that is unfamiliar to many Bangladeshi institutions. The website must do the work of establishing that this organisation is:

- **Legitimate:** registered, accountable, with a named leadership team and verifiable partners
- **Research-backed:** grounded in evidence, not opinion (see Section 7)
- **Experienced:** already working with schools, already producing results

**Target audiences for credibility:**
- School principals and administrators (Class 1–5 focus)
- Classroom teachers and curriculum coordinators
- Parents of primary school children
- Domestic and international donors and funders
- Government education bodies and media

### 1.2 Secondary Objective — Drive School Partnership Enquiries

The immediate commercial and programmatic goal of the website is to generate enquiries from schools interested in adopting the Blu Foundation curriculum or teacher-training programme. Every school that visits the site should be nudged, at multiple points, toward a clear and frictionless enquiry form.

### 1.3 Tertiary Objectives

In rough order of priority after the primary and secondary goals:

1. **Online course enrolment** — directing parents and teachers toward the digital learning offer
2. **Donation** — converting donors, both one-off and recurring
3. **Newsletter signup** — building a low-friction contact list across all audience segments

## 5. Content Tone and Style Guide

### 5.1 Core Tone

The Blu Foundation voice is:

- **Warm, not clinical.** We speak like a trusted teacher, not a policy document.
- **Aspirational, not preachy.** We believe in children's potential. We do not lecture parents.
- **Evidence-backed, not academic.** We cite research, but we translate it into plain language.
- **Honest about scale.** We are growing. We do not overclaim. We let our evidence speak.
- **Specific, not vague.** "We have reached 1,200 children in 14 schools in Dhaka and Chattogram" is always better than "We have reached thousands of children across Bangladesh."

### 5.2 Language and Bilingual Approach

**Bengali-first contexts:** Parent-facing content (parent guides, newsletter, community programme descriptions, social media for parents). Bengali text appears first; English translation follows in smaller type or in a toggle.

**English-first contexts:** Donor-facing content (donation page, annual report, international partnership pages), media-facing content (press releases, media kit). English appears first; Bengali translation available via language toggle.

**Mixed contexts:** Home page, About page, Research page — lead with English, make Bengali toggle prominent. These pages serve all audiences.

**Language toggle:** A prominent Bengali/English toggle must appear in the header navigation on all pages. Default language should be detected by browser locale where possible. [FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm whether Bengali or English should be the site default for visitors without a detected locale preference.]

**Transliteration note:** Where Bangladeshi place names, people's names, or cultural terms appear, use standard English transliterations consistently (e.g., "Dhaka" not "Dhaka City"; "Chattogram" not "Chittagong"). [FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm preferred transliterations for any organisation-specific terms.]

### 5.3 Photography and Visual Style

**Photography brief:**
- **Subject:** Children aged 5–12, diverse in appearance, background, and school type (urban private, rural government)
- **Setting:** Classrooms, school courtyards, community spaces — real Bangladesh environments, not international stock photo settings
- **Dress:** School uniforms or modest everyday dress appropriate to Bangladeshi norms; no Western-style casual clothing for children unless contextually appropriate
- **Expression:** Natural, engaged, joyful — not posed "model" smiles
- **Never use:** Stock photos featuring non-Bangladeshi children, or AI-generated images of children

**Video brief:** Short (60–90 second) clips for the homepage hero and programme pages. Clips should show a classroom in session, children engaged in etiquette activities, and a teacher speaking (with subtitles in both English and Bengali). [NEEDS BENGALI TRANSLATION: all video subtitle text]

**Colour palette:** Use Blu Foundation's established brand colours consistently. [FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm official hex colour codes for primary and secondary brand colours, logo file formats.]

### 5.4 Data and Citations

- Every statistic cited on the website must reference its source code (SOURCE_BD_01–25 or SOURCE_GL_01–05) in the content management system's back-end metadata, even when the visible text on-page does not show the full citation.
- Where a statistic is approximated or rounded (e.g. "research shows SEL improves academic performance by around 11 percentile points"), the original source must still be logged.
- Avoid statistics older than five years unless they are foundational (e.g., landmark meta-analyses). Check all statistics for Bangladesh-specific relevance before publication.

## 7. The Problem Page — Research Evidence Section

*The following is proposed final web copy for the Research & Evidence > The Problem page. It is written at approximately 370 words in accessible, jargon-free English. A parallel Bengali version is required for publication. [NEEDS BENGALI TRANSLATION]*

---

### Why Manners Matter — and Why Schools Must Teach Them

Something has changed in Bangladesh's classrooms.

The academic pressure on primary school children — to pass tests, score marks, and move up through a competitive system — has never been higher. But in the race to teach what can be measured, something essential is being left behind: the skills children need to treat others well, navigate conflict peacefully, and become citizens their communities can be proud of. [SOURCE_BD_01]

Research conducted in Bangladesh's schools has documented a troubling decline in ethical values and prosocial behaviour among young people. [SOURCE_BD_15] This is not a failure of individual families. It is a structural gap: values education has been squeezed out of the curriculum, and neither teachers nor schools have been given the tools to address it.

**Bullying is more common than most parents realise.**

Studies on peer conduct in Bangladeshi primary schools have found significant rates of bullying behaviour — physical, verbal, and social — affecting children as young as six and seven years old. [SOURCE_BD_09] Behind these numbers are real children: the child who dreads the walk to school, the child who has no one to sit with at lunch, the child whose confidence is being eroded every day.

**The global evidence is clear.**

Decades of international research on social-emotional learning (SEL) — the structured teaching of interpersonal skills, self-management, and empathy — shows consistent results. A landmark analysis of over 200 school-based SEL programmes found that students who received structured social-emotional learning improved their academic performance by an average of 11 percentile points compared to students who did not. [SOURCE_GL_01] This is not a marginal gain. It is the difference between a child who struggles and a child who thrives.

Studies in school settings across multiple countries have demonstrated that character education, delivered systematically and with trained teachers, produces lasting improvements in student behaviour, classroom environment, and community relationships. [SOURCE_UK_01]

**This is the gap Blu Foundation exists to close.**

We bring structured, evidence-based manners and etiquette education into the primary school classroom — so that children learn not just to read and write, but to listen, share, and treat others with dignity.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### 9.1 Device and Connectivity

- **Mobile-first design is non-negotiable.** Bangladesh has among the highest mobile web usage rates in South Asia, with over 90% of web traffic from mobile devices [SOURCE_BD_13]. All layouts must be designed for a 375px screen width first, then scaled up to tablet and desktop.
- **3G optimisation:** The site must load its above-the-fold content within 3 seconds on a simulated 3G connection (approximately 1.6 Mbps download). This requires:
  - WebP image format with JPEG fallback
  - Lazy loading for all below-fold images and video thumbnails
  - Minified CSS and JavaScript
  - Content Delivery Network (CDN) with a server node in South or Southeast Asia
  - Hero images compressed to under 150kb

### 9.2 Language / Internationalisation

- **Language toggle:** Persistent Bengali/English toggle in header navigation. State preserved across page navigation via cookie or local storage.
- **URL structure:** Recommend single-URL internationalisation with `lang` parameter (e.g., `?lang=bn`) rather than separate subdomain or subdirectory, to consolidate SEO authority on a single domain.
- **Font stack:** Hind Siliguri or SolaimanLipi for Bengali text. Ensure Bengali glyphs render correctly on Android system fonts (the most common mobile OS in Bangladesh). Test on Samsung Internet browser.

### 9.3 Accessibility

- **WCAG 2.1 Level AA minimum compliance.** This is the international standard and should be treated as a floor, not a ceiling.
- Specific requirements:
  - All images have descriptive alt text in both English and Bengali `[NEEDS BENGALI TRANSLATION]`
  - Colour contrast ratio minimum 4.5:1 for body text
  - All forms are keyboard-navigable
  - Video content has captions (Bengali and English) `[NEEDS BENGALI TRANSLATION]`
  - No content conveyed by colour alone

### 9.4 Security and Privacy

- **SSL/TLS certificate:** HTTPS on all pages. Required for bKash and card payment integration. Non-negotiable.
- **Privacy policy:** A Bangladesh-compliant privacy policy covering:
  - Collection of children's data (the website may display photos of minors; consent procedures must be documented)
  - Newsletter data handling
  - Cookie consent (required for Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel)
  - Data retention policy
  - Contact for data removal requests
  `[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: legal counsel review of privacy policy before launch. Template to be provided in a separate deliverable.]`
- **GDPR note:** If the site accepts donations from EU-based visitors (e.g., Bangladeshi diaspora in the UK or Germany), a GDPR-compliant cookie consent banner is required. Recommend a tool such as Cookiebot or a simple custom implementation.

### 9.5 Analytics and Tracking

- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Install on all pages. Configure conversion events for: form submissions (school enquiry, contact, newsletter), donation page views, donation button clicks, language toggle usage.
- **Facebook Pixel:** Install for retargeting of visitors who viewed the Donate page or the Partner With Us page. `[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm whether a Facebook Business Manager account exists or needs to be created.]`
- **Heatmapping (recommended):** Microsoft Clarity (free) or Hotjar (paid) to understand how visitors interact with the home page and donation page. Particularly useful for identifying where mobile users drop off.

### 9.6 Hosting and Infrastructure

- **Recommended hosting:** A managed WordPress host (Kinsta, WP Engine, or SiteGround) with a server in Singapore or Mumbai (lowest latency for Bangladesh). Alternatively, a static site generator (Next.js or Astro) deployed on Vercel or Cloudflare Pages offers better performance but requires more development expertise.
- **Domain:** `[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm primary domain name — blu-foundation.org.bd (Bangladesh ccTLD, adds local trust) versus blu-foundation.org (international, easier for donors outside Bangladesh).]`
- **Email:** Professional email on the same domain (e.g., info@blu-foundation.org). Google Workspace recommended for ease of use. `[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED: confirm email domain and setup preference.]`
- **Backup:** Daily automated backups. Off-site backup to a separate cloud storage provider (e.g., AWS S3 or Backblaze B2).
