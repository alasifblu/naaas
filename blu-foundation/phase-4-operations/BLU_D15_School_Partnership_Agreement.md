## RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**

A. Blu Foundation is an organisation dedicated to manners and etiquette education for primary school children in Bangladesh, with a mission to build character, respect, and civic values among young learners in Classes 1 to 5.

B. Blu Foundation has developed a structured Manners and Etiquette Curriculum, comprising age-appropriate textbooks, teacher guides, worksheets, and digital learning content, designed for integration into the timetables of primary schools.

C. The School is a primary educational institution committed to the holistic development of its students and wishes to incorporate the Blu Foundation Program into its academic activities.

D. The Parties wish to formalise their relationship and set out their respective rights and obligations in connection with the delivery of the Program at the School.

### 3. Blu Foundation Obligations

**3.1 Materials:** Provide printed textbooks and related Materials to the School at no cost (or at the agreed cost specified in Schedule B), in the quantities set out in Schedule B, within [30] days of the Commencement Date or the start of the relevant academic term, whichever is applicable.

**3.6 Termly Progress Reports:** Provide the School with a written termly progress report summarising Program delivery, student engagement (based on anonymized data), and recommendations for the following term.

**3.7 Branding and Attribution:** Ensure that Blu Foundation's involvement with the School is communicated accurately and respectfully in any public communications, reports, or promotional materials that reference the partnership.

### 4. School Obligations

The School shall:

**4.1 Timetable Integration:** Integrate the Program into the School timetable in accordance with Clause 2.3. The School shall make reasonable efforts to maintain the agreed minimum number of periods per week throughout the academic term.

**4.2 Program Coordinator:** Designate a named member of teaching staff as the Program Coordinator for the duration of the Term. The name and contact details of the Program Coordinator shall be recorded in Schedule B. The School shall notify Blu Foundation in writing within 10 working days if the Program Coordinator changes.

**4.3 Teacher Training Attendance:** Ensure that all designated Teachers attend the teacher training workshop provided by Blu Foundation under Clause 3.2. The School acknowledges that only trained Teachers are authorised to deliver the Program.

**4.4 Monitoring Visits:** Permit Blu Foundation staff and authorised representatives to conduct monitoring visits in accordance with Clause 3.5. During any monitoring visit, Blu Foundation visitors must be accompanied by the Program Coordinator or another member of school staff at all times.

**4.5 Student Data:** Permit anonymized Student Data to be collected and shared with Blu Foundation for monitoring, evaluation, and research purposes, in accordance with Clause 6 and Schedule D. The School confirms that it shall ensure data is anonymized before sharing and shall not share any personally identifiable student information without specific prior written consent.

**4.6 Branding:** Display Blu Foundation branding materials (including logos and program signage as provided by Blu Foundation) in appropriate locations within the School, such as classrooms used for Program delivery and notice boards. Blu Foundation shall not require the School to display branding in a manner that conflicts with the School's own policies.

**4.7 Annual Feedback Report:** Provide Blu Foundation with a written feedback report at the end of each Academic Year, in the format agreed with Blu Foundation or in the template provided by Blu Foundation, summarising the School's experience of the Program, challenges encountered, and suggestions for improvement.

**4.8 Materials — No Unauthorised Use:** Not copy, reproduce, redistribute, sell, lend, or otherwise deal in the Materials without the prior written consent of Blu Foundation, in accordance with Clause 5.

**4.9 Child Safeguarding:** Comply with Blu Foundation's Child Safeguarding Policy (as notified to the School) and applicable Bangladesh laws and government guidelines concerning child welfare and school safety throughout the Term.

**4.10 Communication:** Ensure reasonable responsiveness to communications from Blu Foundation's program team and respond to requests for information or data within 10 working days where reasonably practicable.

### 6. Data and Privacy

**6.1 Anonymization:** All Student Data shared with Blu Foundation under this Agreement must be fully anonymized prior to sharing. Anonymized data means data from which no individual student can be directly or indirectly identified. The School is responsible for ensuring anonymization before sharing.

**6.2 Prohibition on Sharing Personal Data:** Neither the School nor Blu Foundation shall share, publish, or disclose any personally identifiable information about students — including names, photographs, audio recordings, or video footage — without specific prior written consent from the student's parent or guardian, using the consent form set out in Schedule C or an equivalent form approved by Blu Foundation.

**6.3 Blu Foundation's Use of Anonymized Data:** Blu Foundation may use aggregated, anonymized Student Data for the following purposes:

- (a) monitoring and evaluating Program effectiveness;
- (b) preparing internal and donor reports;
- (c) academic research and publication (in aggregated form only);
- (d) fundraising and grant applications, provided no individual school or student is identifiable.

**6.4 Photo and Video Consent:** Any photography or video recording of students or staff at the School by Blu Foundation or its representatives requires separate written consent from the relevant parent or guardian in advance. The template parental consent form is set out in Schedule C. Blu Foundation shall not publish or publicly share any image or recording in which a student is identifiable without such consent.

**6.5 Data Security:** Both Parties shall take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect any data shared under this Agreement from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

**6.6 Applicable Law:** Both Parties agree to comply with applicable Bangladesh laws and regulations concerning data protection, privacy, and the protection of minors, including any requirements imposed by the Ministry of Education or relevant government authorities. Where specific data protection legislation is enacted in Bangladesh that applies to the activities under this Agreement, both Parties shall update their practices accordingly.

**6.7 Data Sharing Protocol:** The format, frequency, and method of data sharing between the Parties is described in Schedule D.

### 9. Termination

**9.1 Mutual Consent:** This Agreement may be terminated at any time by the written agreement of both Parties.

**9.2 Termination for Cause — Immediate:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice to the other Party if the other Party:

- (a) engages in any conduct that causes or risks harm to a child or children involved in or connected with the Program;
- (b) is found to have acted in violation of applicable child safeguarding standards or laws;
- (c) engages in conduct that brings the other Party's name or reputation into serious disrepute;
- (d) becomes insolvent, is subject to dissolution, or (in the case of Blu Foundation) ceases charitable or NGO operations.

**9.3 Consequences of Termination:** Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason:

- (a) The School shall promptly return to Blu Foundation, or certifiably destroy, all unused Materials in its possession within 30 days of the termination date;
- (b) The license granted to the School under Clause 5.2 shall immediately cease;
- (c) Blu Foundation shall retain all Student Data already collected and anonymized in accordance with this Agreement;
- (d) Any financial obligations accrued up to the termination date shall remain due and payable;
- (e) Clauses 5 (Intellectual Property), 6 (Data and Privacy), 11 (Dispute Resolution), and 12.4 (Governing Law) shall survive termination.

**9.4 No Compensation on Termination:** Neither Party shall be entitled to compensation from the other Party solely by reason of the expiry or termination of this Agreement, except in respect of obligations that accrued prior to termination.

**10.5 Blu Foundation Visitors:** Blu Foundation staff, volunteers, or other representatives visiting the School under this Agreement must:

**10.6 Disclosure of Concern:** Either Party shall promptly disclose to the other Party any safeguarding concern, allegation, or incident connected with the Program of which it becomes aware, subject to applicable legal constraints.

### 11. Dispute Resolution

**11.1 Good-Faith Negotiation:** In the event of any dispute, disagreement, or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement (including its formation, validity, interpretation, performance, or termination), the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith negotiation between their authorised representatives.

**11.2 Notice of Dispute:** The Party wishing to raise a dispute shall give written notice to the other Party describing the nature and particulars of the dispute. The Parties shall then endeavour to resolve the dispute by negotiation within **30 days** of such notice being received (or such longer period as the Parties may agree in writing).

**11.3 Mediation:** If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation within the period specified in Clause 11.2, either Party may refer the dispute to mediation. The mediator shall be a neutral third party agreed by both Parties in writing. The costs of mediation shall be shared equally between the Parties unless otherwise agreed.

**11.4 Jurisdiction:** If mediation is unsuccessful or if either Party declines to participate in mediation, the dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Bangladesh. The Parties irrevocably submit to the jurisdiction of those courts for the resolution of any dispute that cannot be resolved pursuant to Clauses 11.1 to 11.3.

**11.5 Continued Performance:** Unless a dispute relates to payment, both Parties shall continue to perform their respective obligations under this Agreement during any dispute resolution process.

## SCHEDULE A — Program Description

**Title:** Blu Foundation Manners and Etiquette Curriculum

**Overview:**
The Blu Foundation Program is a structured character and etiquette education curriculum designed for primary school children in Bangladesh, covering Classes 1 to 5. The Program aims to build foundational social skills, respectful conduct, civic values, and personal responsibility in young learners through age-appropriate, interactive content.

**Curriculum Structure:**

| Class | Age Range (approx.) | Core Themes |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | 6–7 years | Greetings, saying please and thank you, tidiness, listening skills |
| Class 2 | 7–8 years | Respecting elders, classroom behaviour, sharing, basic table manners |
| Class 3 | 8–9 years | Punctuality, caring for property, kindness, community respect |
| Class 4 | 9–10 years | Conflict resolution, personal responsibility, cultural respect, online manners (introductory) |
| Class 5 | 10–11 years | Leadership through example, empathy, civic participation, responsible communication |

**Materials Included in the Program:**

- Student Textbook (one per enrolled student, per class level)
- Teacher Guide (one per Teacher delivering the Program, per class level)
- Student Worksheets and Activity Sheets (consumable; quantity per Schedule B)
- Termly Student Assessment Rubric
- Digital access to Blu Foundation Online Platform (where available)

**Delivery Model:**
The Program is designed to be delivered by class teachers who have completed the Blu Foundation two-day teacher training workshop. Sessions are activity-based and interactive. The curriculum is designed to complement, not replace, existing subjects and can be delivered as a standalone subject period or integrated across subjects at the School's discretion.

**Assessment:**
Student progress is assessed through informal observation rubrics and simple end-of-term assessments. Assessments are formative and are designed to support learning, not rank students. Anonymized assessment data is shared with Blu Foundation for program evaluation purposes under Clause 6 and Schedule D.

## SCHEDULE D — Data Sharing Protocol

*This Schedule sets out the type, format, frequency, and method of data sharing between the School and Blu Foundation under Clause 6.*

**1. What Data Will Be Shared**

The School will share the following anonymized data with Blu Foundation:

| Data Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Class-level enrollment | Number of students enrolled in the Program per class per term (no individual names) |
| Attendance data | Number (not names) of students attending Program sessions each week or term |
| Assessment results | Aggregated class-level scores or rubric outcomes from termly assessments (no individual scores linked to named students) |
| Teacher training completion | Names and dates of training completed by Teachers (staff data — not student data) |
| Monitoring visit notes | Blu Foundation's own monitoring notes from school visits |

**2. What Data Will NOT Be Shared**

The following data will not be shared with Blu Foundation under any circumstances unless specific written consent is obtained:

- Individual student names
- Individual student assessment scores linked to a named student
- Student identification numbers
- Student photographs or video (without Schedule C consent)
- Parent or guardian contact information
- Any health, special needs, or sensitive personal information relating to individual students

**3. Format**

Data shall be submitted in the format agreed between the Program Coordinator and the Blu Foundation Program Manager. Acceptable formats include:

- Simple Excel or Google Sheets template (provided by Blu Foundation)
- PDF summary report
- Paper form (to be scanned or photographed and emailed)

Blu Foundation shall provide template data collection forms to the School within 14 days of the Commencement Date.

**4. Frequency**

Data shall be shared at the following intervals:

| Data Type | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Enrollment numbers | Once per term (at the start of each term) |
| Attendance summary | Once per term (at the end of each term) |
| Assessment results | Once per term (within 4 weeks of end of term) |
| Annual feedback report | Once per year (within 6 weeks of end of Academic Year) |

**5. Method of Transmission**

Data may be transmitted by:

- Email to the Blu Foundation Program Manager's email address (recorded in Schedule B)
- Shared secure cloud folder (if set up by mutual agreement)
- In person, during a monitoring visit

Paper records containing any student data (even anonymized) should not be sent by unregistered post.

**6. Data Retention**

Blu Foundation shall retain anonymized data collected under this Agreement for a period of [5] years from the date of collection, for program evaluation and research purposes. After this period, data will be securely deleted unless required for ongoing research with appropriate ethical approval.

**7. Queries**

Any queries regarding data sharing under this Schedule should be directed to:

- **Blu Foundation:** [Program Manager name and email — from Schedule B]
- **School:** Program Coordinator [name and email — from Schedule B]
