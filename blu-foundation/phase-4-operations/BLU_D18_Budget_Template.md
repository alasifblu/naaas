## 1. Overview and Assumptions

### 1.1 Programme Context

Blu Foundation is a Bangladesh-based NGO whose mission is to teach manners, etiquette, and pro-social behaviour to primary school children in Classes 1–5. This budget template covers the first three fiscal years of structured programme delivery, from an initial two-school pilot through to a fifty-school scale phase reaching an estimated 10,000 students.

### 1.2 Fiscal Year Structure

Bangladesh government schools operate on a **January–December academic calendar**, but the **NGO fiscal year in this plan follows July–June** to align with typical donor reporting cycles and NGOAB (NGO Affairs Bureau) financial year conventions. All annual totals below refer to July–June fiscal years.

| Fiscal Year | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| July 2026 – June 2027 | **Year 1 (FY2026–27)** | Pilot Phase |
| July 2027 – June 2028 | **Year 2 (FY2027–28)** | Expansion Phase |
| July 2028 – June 2029 | **Year 3 (FY2028–29)** | Scale Phase |

### 1.3 Programme Scale Assumptions

| Parameter | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schools enrolled | 2 | 10 | 50 |
| Students reached | ~200 | ~1,000 | ~10,000 |
| Teachers trained | 10 | 50 | 250 |
| Average school size | 100 students | 100 students | 200 students |
| Teachers per school | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Class grades covered | 1–3 (pilot) | 1–5 | 1–5 |
| Classes per grade per school | 2 | 2 | 2–4 |

> **[FOUNDER INPUT REQUIRED]** Confirm year-on-year school growth targets (2 → 10 → 50). If targets differ, all volume-driven line items below must be recalculated proportionally.

### 1.4 Currency and Exchange Rate

- All budget figures are expressed in **Bangladeshi Taka (BDT)**.
- For donor reference and grant applications, figures are also converted to **United States Dollars (USD)**.
- Conversion rate used throughout: **approximately 1 USD = 110 BDT** (2026 indicative estimate).
- **This rate is approximate and subject to exchange rate fluctuation.** Verify the prevailing Bangladesh Bank mid-rate at the time of each transaction or grant application.
- Large figures are also expressed in **lakh (1 lakh = 100,000 BDT)** and **crore (1 crore = 10,000,000 BDT)** following standard Bangladesh convention.

### 1.5 Contingency Policy

A contingency allowance of **15%** has been added to all direct programme costs (Sections A through G in each year). Contingency does not apply to staff salaries or recurring operational overhead, which are estimated with their own variance ranges.
